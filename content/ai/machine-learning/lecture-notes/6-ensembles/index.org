* Rule Pruning 
- Two approaches to pruning:
    - Prune whole rules
    - Prune predicates within rules
    
- Pruning predicates subsumes pruning rules (you dont need to do both)
- Sort rules by coverage 
- Resulting pruning strategy, similar to decision trees is Incremental Reduced Error Pruning (IREP)
