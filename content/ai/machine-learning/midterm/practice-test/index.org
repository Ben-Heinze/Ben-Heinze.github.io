The whole midterm in one place: every topic from Weeks 1-5, the four Alpaydin
chapters, and both programming assignments. Pick a unit and a length, press
*Start timer*, and work down the page. *Reveal answer* checks you against the
model answer one question at a time -- pseudocode questions reveal a worked
outline underneath it -- and *New test* reshuffles into a different set with
freshly randomized numbers on the calculations. Switch *Mode* to *Flashcards* to
run the same pool one card at a time.

Everything is self-assessed. Nothing is auto-graded, including the calculations:
the engine shows the answer and the worked steps, and you mark your own. Leave
*+calculations* checked to mix the randomized numeric drills in with the
conceptual questions.

The pool is the full [[file:question-bank/][Question Bank]] -- all eleven
machine-learning units, which is also what keeps the Operating Systems questions
out. Anything added to a bank page shows up here automatically. For a narrower
session, use the per-topic tests:

[[file:clustering-and-dimensionality/][Clustering and Dimensionality]],

[[file:project-walkthroughs/][Project Walkthroughs]].
