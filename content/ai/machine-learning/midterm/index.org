Study hub for the *CSCI 447 midterm, October 16 2026*. Closed book, no aids. The
syllabus says the exam has three parts:

1. Objective-style questions
2. {{{hl(blue,Specific questions on the programming assignments)}}}
3. Open-ended short answer

The practice material below is shaped to match that: mostly free-response with a
reveal-the-model-answer self-grade, a layer of objective questions for sharp
recall, randomized numeric drills for the calculations, and a whole section of
project questions on mechanism and pseudocode.

The one explicit instruction from the course so far is on the
[[file:../][Machine Learning]] index page: *what to study: the Winnow
algorithm*. There are twelve Winnow questions in the foundations bank,
including both update rules, the worked weight trace, the representation bias,
pseudocode and a randomized weight-update drill.

* Where to start

- [[file:practice-test/][Practice Test]] -- the whole midterm, every unit. Pick a
  length, start the timer, work down the page. This is the one to use for a full
  sitting.

mark your own. Every test also has a *Flashcards* mode that runs the same pool
one card at a time.

* Topic checklist

Work through this and look up anything you cannot state from memory. Each line
links to the notes it comes from.

** [[file:../lecture-notes/1-intro-to-ml/][Week 1: Intro to ML]]
- Mitchell's and Alpaydin's definitions of learning
- Supervised (critic / learner / actor), unsupervised, reinforcement, semi-supervised; online vs offline vs active
- *Winnow-2*: model, threshold rule, promotion, demotion, the weight trace, linear representation bias
- Inductive bias: the formal definition, representation vs preference bias, the unbiased-learner theorem
- No Free Lunch; the Inductive Learning Hypothesis
- True error vs sample error
- The risk function and its seven symbols; the five loss functions
- The bias-variance decomposition, both steps; what \(\lambda\) controls

** [[file:../lecture-notes/2-nonparametric-learning/][Week 2: Nonparametric Learning]]
- Classification as density estimation
- Parzen windows; the Parzen-versus-k-NN duality; Voronoi diagrams
- The four metric axioms; Minkowski with \(p = 1, 2, \infty\); VDM
- Histogram, naive, weighted, kernel and k-NN density estimators; the binomial derivation
- The three kernel properties; the feature map
- The k-NN posterior \(k_i / k\)
- Edited NN v1 and v2, condensed NN, KD-trees and the pruning test
- Regressogram, running mean and kernel smoothers; null models

** [[file:../lecture-notes/3-density-estimation/][Week 3: Density Estimation]]
- LOF and one-class classification; the four sources of NN error
- Filter vs wrapper; stepwise forward and backward selection
- Hill climbing (simple and stochastic), simulated annealing, genetic algorithms
- PCA: centering, covariance, SVD, what \(W\) and \(D\) hold, proportion of variance
- Fisher's LDA: \(J(w)\), \(S_B\), \(S_W\), and \(w = S_W^{-1}(m_1 - m_2)\)
- EM for clustering: the E step and the M step; k-means; k-means++ and its theorem

** [[file:../lecture-notes/4-learning-theory/][Week 4: Learning Theory]]
- k-medoids, PAM, fuzzy c-means, leader clustering
- Cohesion and separation; SSE and SSB; pseudo-F; silhouette; Rand and ARI; Dunn
- Version spaces, the \(S\)/\(G\) representation, candidate elimination, the EnjoySport trace
- Sample complexity; active learning and \(\lg|\mathcal{H}|\); the \(n+1\) result
- The Haussler bound and its proof; \(m \geq \frac{1}{\epsilon}(\ln|\mathcal{H}| + \ln\frac{1}{\delta})\)
- Conjunctions of literals (\(|\mathcal{H}| = 3^{n}\)); agnostic learning; the unbiased learner
- PAC learning; efficiently PAC-learnable
- VC dimension, shattering, the VC table, both sample-complexity bounds, the triple tradeoff
- Risk minimization, 0/1 loss, \(h_{MAP}\) and \(h_{ML}\)

** [[file:../lecture-notes/5-tree-learning/][Week 5: Tree Learning]]
- ID3, entropy, expected entropy, information gain; the Quinlan weather numbers
- Gain ratio and intrinsic value; why unique identifiers break plain gain
- Continuous attributes: binary splits, midpoints, quantiles, equal-width vs equal-frequency
- Regression trees: the MSE criterion, leaf means, MLE implies least squares, small disjuncts
- Overfitting (formally); reduced error pruning; early stopping and Quinlan's chi-square
- Preference vs representation bias; oblique trees and OC1
- Propositional logic, the nine inference rules, CNF/DNF/Horn
- FOL, quantifiers, unification, Skolem constants; resolution theorem proving
- Sequential covering; FoilGain; rule pruning and IREP

** Textbook
- [[file:../textbook-notes/1-intro-to-ml/][Ch. 1]] -- association rules, discriminants, predictive vs descriptive models
- [[file:../textbook-notes/7-clustering/][Ch. 7]] -- mixture densities, vector quantization, spectral and hierarchical clustering, linkage, choosing \(k\)
- [[file:../textbook-notes/8-nonparametric-methods/][Ch. 8]] -- the parametric/nonparametric contrast, kernel estimators, curse of dimensionality
- [[file:../textbook-notes/20-designs-and-experiments/][Ch. 20]] -- the four cautions, strategies of experimentation, randomization/replication/blocking, K-fold, 5x2, bootstrap, ROC and AUC

** [[file:../projects/][Projects]]
- Null models; min-max vs z-score; why normalization precedes the metric
- VDM; combining numeric with categorical distance; why the \(p\)th root can be dropped
- \(k = 1\) during editing and condensing, tuned \(k\) at prediction; the RBF kernel and \(\gamma\)
- Per-dataset traps: ERP on Computer Hardware, cyclic month/day and the log transform on Forest Fires, "?" meaning abstain on Congressional Vote, identifier columns everywhere
- Gain ratio and MSE splitting criteria; reduced error pruning; the 20% pruning holdout inside 5x2 CV
