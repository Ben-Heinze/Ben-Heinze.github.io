* How the questions are built

Most are *free response*: you recall the answer, then *Reveal answer* shows the
model answer and you mark yourself. A thinner layer of multiple-choice,
true/false and fill-in questions covers the definitions worth knowing cold --
the metric axioms, the VC-dimension table, the linkage types, which loss suits
which output.

*Pseudocode questions* reveal a monospace outline under the model answer. In
those outlines, *leading dots mark indentation depth*, since the single-line Org
property that carries them cannot hold real indentation. They cover the
algorithms worth being able to write from memory: Winnow-2, k-NN classification
and regression, edited and condensed NN, KD-tree build and query, LOF, hill
climbing, simulated annealing, genetic algorithms, PCA, Fisher's LDA, k-means,
k-means++, EM, fuzzy c-means, agglomerative clustering, silhouette, candidate
elimination, ID3, regression trees, numeric split selection, reduced error
pruning, resolution, sequential covering, K-fold and 5x2 cross-validation, and
both project pipelines end to end.

*Calculation questions* are generated fresh on every =New test=, so the numbers
change but the method does not. They cover entropy and information gain, gain
ratio, precision/recall/F-beta, micro versus macro averaging, the Haussler and
PAC and VC sample-complexity bounds, the silhouette coefficient, pseudo-F, LOF,
Minkowski distance, VDM, a Winnow-2 weight update, a Gaussian KDE, the k-NN
posterior, the bootstrap percentages, and a regression-tree leaf MSE. Each one
reveals its worked steps so you can check your arithmetic against the method.

* Adding to the bank

Add a level-2 heading on the relevant page with a =:Q_TYPE:= drawer and it shows
up on every test that includes its =:Q_UNIT:=, with no other edits needed:

#+begin_example
,** Write the gain ratio and explain what it guards against.
:PROPERTIES:
:Q_TYPE: short
:Q_ANSWER: gainRatio(f) = gain(f) / IV(f) ...
:Q_STEPS: STEP-ONE(...): | .  pipe-separated lines render as a monospace block
:Q_UNIT: trees
:END:
#+end_example
