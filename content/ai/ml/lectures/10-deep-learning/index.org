* Hopfield Networks


Auto-associative network with symmetric weights. There are no self-recurrent loops. Every edge 


one-shot learning: If you take a particular pattern, take its outer product, then subtract the identity matrix, you'll get a new matrix

\[
  \mathbf{w}_p = \mathbf{x}_p \mathbf{x}_p^T  - \text{Identity matrix}
\]
\[
  \mathbf{W} = \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \mathbf{w}_p
\]

* Energy in hopfield networks

- let $h_i$ be the activation level of node $i$
- the activation levels are calcualted as:
\[
  h_i = \sum_{j} w_{ij} x_j
\]


Define energy of a node $i$ as:

\[
  E_i = - \frac{1}{2} h_i x_i
\]

Energy of the network is then

\[
  E(x) = \sum_{i} E_i = - \sum_{i} \frac{1}{2} h_i x_i = - \frac{1}{2} \sum_{ij} w_{ij} x_i x_j - \sum_{i} \theta_i x_i
\]

Hebbian learning: if you have two connected nodes that agree on a state, increase the strength of that state. If they disagree, decrease the weight. 
The outer product has a Hebbian learning aspect to it: high weights when nodes agree, low weights when nodes disagree
