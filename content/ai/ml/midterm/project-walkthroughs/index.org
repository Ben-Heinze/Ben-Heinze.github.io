The syllabus says one of the three parts of the exam is *specific questions on
the programming assignments*. This is that part: *Project 1* (k-NN) and
*Project 2* (decision trees), on mechanism rather than code.

Null models and what they are actually for, min-max versus z-score and why
normalization has to come first, the Value Difference Metric, the rule for
combining numeric with categorical distance and the reason the pth root can be
dropped, k = 1 during editing and condensing against a tuned k at prediction,
the RBF kernel and its bandwidth, the per-dataset traps -- ERP on Computer
Hardware, cyclic month distance and the log transform on Forest Fires, "?"
meaning abstain on Congressional Vote, the discarded identifier columns -- the
gain ratio and MSE splitting criteria, reduced error pruning, and the 20%
pruning holdout nested inside 5x2 cross-validation.

Several questions ask for pseudocode: the two null models, the mixed-type
distance, cyclic distance, both tree-growing routines, and both complete
experimental pipelines. *Reveal answer* gives the model answer and then the
worked outline underneath it. Self-assessed throughout.
