The sidebar is a tree, so each page sits in exactly one place. Tags are the other
way through the wiki: a page lists the subjects it touches, and any two pages
sharing a subject are linked no matter which sections they live in.

Pick a tag to see every page carrying it, or open the *Graph* to see the whole
wiki laid out by subject: every tagged page is a point, and two pages are joined
when they share a tag — the more tags in common, the stronger the pull between
them. Drag things around; click a page to go to it.

A page's own tags are the chips under its title, and you can filter search with
=tag:name= from anywhere — press ~Ctrl~/~Cmd~ + ~K~ and try =tag:probability=.
