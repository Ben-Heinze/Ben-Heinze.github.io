The original 27-question memory-management quiz, rendered interactively. Pick your
answers, start the timer, then hit *Reveal answer* on a question to check yourself
against it. *Show all answers* reveals the lot at once, and *New test* shuffles for
another run. Grading is yours to do: the engine only shows you the answer.

The questions are authored in the [[file:../test-1/question-bank/][Question Bank]] (tag =exam=) and rendered here by
the shared quiz engine (=#+QUIZ_TEST: exam=). See also the [[file:answer-key/][Answer Key]],
[[file:practice-quiz/][Practice Quiz]], and the [[file:../test-1/][Test 1]] study guide.
