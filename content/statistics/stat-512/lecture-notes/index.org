* Chapters 18-19: odds ratios

From page 7 of the chapter 18-19 notes. A 95% confidence interval for the odds
ratio $\phi$ is built on the log scale and then back-transformed:

$$\log(\hat{\phi}) \pm 1.96\, SE\left(\log(\hat{\phi})\right)$$

95% CI for $\phi$: (1.486, 19.470)

We are 95% confident that the odds of the cancer group is between 1.49 and 19.47
times more likely than those who do not smoke.
