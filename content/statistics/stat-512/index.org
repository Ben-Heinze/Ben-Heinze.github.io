STAT 412/512, Methods of Data Analysis, Montana State University, Spring 2026.
Taught by Katharine (Katie) Banner with Lakviru Perera. The textbook is
/The Statistical Sleuth/ (Ramsey & Schafer, 3rd ed.).

* Course goal

Build on STAT X11. By the end of the course, one should be able to:

- effectively and critically evaluate the inferential statements made in research
  papers when related to MLR (including multifactor ANOVA), logistic regression
  (binary and binomial), Poisson log-linear regression, and basic linear mixed
  models (repeated measures models);
- conduct principled analyses (i.e., following recommended workflows), and write
  statistical reports.

* Course structure

- *First hour, MWF:* lecture via boardwork, handouts, slides and software demos,
  plus discussions and activities.
- *Wednesday:* a short quiz, then about half the period on the lab assignment.
  Labs are done and turned in to Gradescope in groups, and usually finished
  outside of class.
- *Software:* R (4.5.1+) and RStudio Desktop.
