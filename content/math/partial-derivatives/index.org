* Partial Derivatives

  A *partial derivative* measures how a multivariable function
  changes as /one/ variable moves, holding the others fixed. For
  $f(x, y)$, the partial derivative with respect to $x$ is

  \[
  \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h, y) - f(x, y)}{h}
  \]

  In practice, just differentiate as usual and treat every other
  variable as a constant. For example, with $f(x, y) = x^2 y + y^3$,

  \[
  \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = 2xy, \qquad \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = x^2 + 3y^2
  \]
