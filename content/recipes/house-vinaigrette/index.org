Blender vinaigrette. The full recipe makes a restaurant-sized batch, so the
1/12 scaling is the one to use at home.

* Ingredients (1/12 batch)

- 1 C rice vinegar
- 1 C pure olive oil
- 1 tsp fresh basil
- 3/4 tsp cane sugar
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper

* Ingredients (full batch)

- 3 qt rice vinegar
- 3 qt pure olive oil
- 1/4 cup fresh basil
- 3 Tbs cane sugar
- 1 Tbs ground black pepper

* Procedure

1. Place all ingredients in a blender and blend.
