Roasted vegetables alongside chicken and pasta. The vegetables are the part
worth writing down; the chicken and pasta are cook-them-however-you-like.

* Ingredients

- Broccoli
- Carrots
- Garlic
- Salt
- Oil
- Your favorite pasta
- Chicken

* Roasted Vegetables

1. Preheat the oven to 400 °F.
2. Cut the broccoli (if necessary).
3. Cut the carrots (if necessary).
4. Crush a few garlic cloves with a knife.
5. Add everything to a mixing bowl with some oil and mix. Salt the vegetables
   to taste.
6. Roast for 15 minutes, turn the pan 180°, then roast for another 10.
7. Done.

* Chicken and Pasta

Cook the chicken however you want — a dedicated recipe may land here later.
The pasta is straightforward; just don't overcook it.
