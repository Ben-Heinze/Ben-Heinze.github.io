Homemade Chipotle bowl. Each component is cooked separately, then combined as
needed in a bowl or a tortilla.

* Total Ingredients

** Produce

- 1 red onion
- 1 sweet onion
- 3 garlic cloves
- 2 bunches cilantro
- 1 lime
- 1 head romaine lettuce
- 1–2 tomatoes
- 2 jalapeños

** Seasonings

- 2 Tbs ancho chile powder (this needs a lot)
- Ground cumin
- Dried oregano
- Salt
- Black pepper

** Other

- 1 bag frozen sweet corn
- Olive oil
- 1 can chipotle in adobo sauce
- Chicken thighs

* Chicken

- 1/2 red onion, coarsely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, coarsely chopped
- 2 Tbs adobo sauce from a can of chipotle in adobo sauce
- 2 Tbs olive oil
- 2 Tbs ancho chile powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 Tbs salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 cup water, or as needed
- Chicken thighs (I use 2–3 packets of Costco chicken)

Add all of the ingredients except the chicken to a blender. Use a fork to poke
holes in the chicken thighs, marinate them in the marinade, then cook the
chicken.

* Cilantro Lime Rice

- 1 c long grain rice
- Chopped cilantro
- Lime juice
- Salt

Cook the rice, then add some cilantro, some lime juice, and salt to taste.

* Corn Salsa

- 1 bag frozen sweet corn, thawed
- 1 jalapeño, deseeded
- 1/3 C red onion
- 1/3 C chopped cilantro
- Juice of half a lime
- Salt to taste

* Pico de Gallo

Halve the amounts below — the full recipe makes a lot. See the
[[file:../pico-de-gallo/index.org][Pico de Gallo]] page for the standalone version.

- 1 lb tomatoes (3–4 medium), diced
- 1 cup chopped onions
- 1 jalapeño pepper, deseeded and finely minced
- 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped
- 2 Tbsp lime juice
- 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

1. In a medium bowl, combine the diced tomatoes, onion, jalapeño pepper and
   chopped cilantro.
2. Stir in the lime juice and lightly season with the salt and black pepper, or
   season to taste. Enjoy right away, or cover and refrigerate overnight.
