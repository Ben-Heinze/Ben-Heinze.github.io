Peppers and onions for fajitas. The garlic goes in off the heat at the end so
it doesn't burn.

* Ingredients

| Amount   | Ingredient              |
|----------+-------------------------|
| 1        | large sweet onion       |
| 1        | large red bell pepper   |
| 1        | large green bell pepper |
| 3 Tbsp   | vegetable oil           |
| a pinch  | kosher salt             |
| 3 cloves | minced garlic           |

* Prep

1. Cut the top and bottom off the onion, then slice thinly for a crescent-moon
   shape.
2. Slice the red and green bell peppers into batons/sticks.
3. Mix the vegetables together.

* Cook

1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add 3 Tbsp of vegetable oil and wait for it to get hot.
3. Add the mixed vegetables to the skillet for 1–2 minutes to sear.
4. Season with kosher salt.
5. Toss every so often.
6. Wait for caramelization. The texture should be soft but still have a little
   bite to it.
7. Turn off the heat.
8. Add 3 cloves of minced garlic and stir.
9. Adjust the salt as needed.
