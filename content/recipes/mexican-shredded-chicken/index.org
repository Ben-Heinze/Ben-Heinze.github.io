From [[https://minimalistbaker.com/1-pan-mexican-shredded-chicken/][Minimalist Baker]]
([[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zN-uWOv5744][video version]]). One pan, and
gluten free (supposedly).

- *Fridge:* 2–3 days
- *Freezer:* 1 month

* Ingredients

- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 Tbsp avocado oil (or other neutral oil)
- 2 (~8 oz) chicken breasts, boneless and skinless
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo sauce — not too much, or the flavor takes over
- 1/4 cup vegetable or chicken broth
- 2 Tbsp lime juice
- Cilantro, if you're feeling it

* Directions

1. Heat a large skillet (cast iron if possible) over medium-high heat. I had it
   around 7 and it burned, so maybe go a little lower.
2. While the pan heats, add half of the salt, cumin, paprika, and chili powder
   to one side of the chicken breasts so it's evenly coated. There should be
   plenty of spice — you should barely be able to see the meat.
3. Once hot, add the oil to the pan, then add the chicken seasoned-side down.
   Add the remaining spices and salt to the unseasoned side. Cook for 4
   minutes.
4. Flip the chicken and lower the heat to low. Top with a lid, but leave it
   slightly off-center so steam can escape. Cook until the center of the
   chicken reaches 165 °F (74 °C) — about 8–10 minutes.
5. You can flip the chicken a couple more times, but once is enough if you
   lower the heat adequately, so the underside doesn't burn.
6. Once the chicken is cooked, turn off the heat and use two forks to shred it
   into bite-sized pieces — as finely as you like. Then add the chipotle pepper
   in adobo sauce, broth, and lime juice. Stir to combine.
7. Turn the heat back to low and cover. Simmer for 5–8 minutes, stirring
   occasionally to blend the flavors, then turn off the heat.
8. Store cooled leftovers covered in the refrigerator for up to 2–3 days
   (assuming the chicken was fresh), or in the freezer up to 1 month. Reheat on
   the stovetop, rehydrating with broth or water as needed.
