From [[https://natashaskitchen.com/pico-de-gallo/][Natasha's Kitchen]]. Makes a lot — halve it if you're feeding
one or two people.

* Ingredients

- 1 lb tomatoes (3–4 medium), diced
- 1 cup chopped onions
- 1 jalapeño pepper, deseeded and finely minced
- 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped
- 2 Tbsp lime juice
- 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

* Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, combine the diced tomatoes, onion, jalapeño pepper and
   chopped cilantro.
2. Stir in the lime juice and lightly season with the salt and black pepper, or
   season to taste. Enjoy right away, or cover and refrigerate overnight.
